Self-hosted web fonts used by the site
======================================

inter-latin-wght.woff2
  Inter (variable, weight axis 100-900), latin subset.
  Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter), SIL Open Font License 1.1, see INTER-OFL.txt

Agrandir (titles) is a commercial font and is not included: add your licensed Agrandir-Regular.woff2 and Agrandir-Bold.woff2 here.

roboto-mono-latin-wght.woff2
  Roboto Mono (variable, weight axis 100-700), latin subset.
  Copyright 2015 The Roboto Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/robotomono)

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
  https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/
The OFL allows the fonts to be used, embedded and redistributed (including
with a website like this one), as long as they are not sold on their own.

Files as packaged by Fontsource (https://fontsource.org), downloaded from
  https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/roboto:vf@latest/latin-wght-normal.woff2
  https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/roboto-mono:vf@latest/latin-wght-normal.woff2

The latin subset covers English and French (accents, œ, « », the euro sign).
Characters outside it (★, ≈, →) fall back to the visitor's system fonts.
